## Built-in settings (no app needed)

**Instagram:** tap the Instagram logo and choose **Following** for only people you follow, newest first (it resets to Home next time). [Instagram](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/favorites-and-following). There is no switch for Reels or Explore; _Reset suggested content_ starts recommendations over. [Instagram Help](https://help.instagram.com/556617736965724/)

**YouTube:** You → Settings → Time management → **Shorts feed limit**, down to zero (adults can dismiss it). [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16671528?hl=en). Turn off **Autoplay** and **Playback in feeds** in Settings.

**Facebook:** no setting removes Reels; _Show less_ lowers similar ones for a while.

**X:** the For You tab cannot be removed or replaced as the default.

**Reddit:** Settings can turn off home-feed recommendations and autoplay.

**iPhone Screen Time:** limits whole apps, so messages are locked too.

## The four ways an iPhone app can block Reels

iOS doesn't let one app change how another app looks, so every Reels blocker uses one of four workarounds. The type matters more than the brand.

| Type                                  | How it works                                                                                                                        | DMs                         | Examples                                                               |
| ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Own app with the platforms inside** | Opens Instagram, YouTube and others inside its own app, with Reels and Shorts removed before you see them                           | Keep working                | StillTabs, FeedOff, minulo, UNDOOMED, SocialLite, Dull, Fluid Friction |
| **Safari extension**                  | Hides Reels and Shorts on the websites, in Safari only; switched on per site in Settings                                            | Work on the website         | Nope, Slowth, #blockit, ScrollGuard                                    |
| **Screen broadcast**                  | You start an iOS broadcast (the screen-recording system) so the app can spot Reels inside the official apps; it has to keep running | Keep working                | Scroll Less (which also opens the sites inside its own app)            |
| **Whole-app blocker**                 | Locks, delays or time-limits the entire app                                                                                         | Locked with everything else | Opal, one sec, ScreenZen, Screen Time                                  |

## How to choose

**"I only want Instagram for messages."** Pick an app of the "own app" type that keeps DMs. Some have a messages-only mode: StillTabs (Blackout mode, free) and minulo (free).

**"I don't want to pay, ever."** StillTabs, FeedOff and Nope have nothing to buy. Many others are free to download but sell a subscription.

**"I keep opening Instagram out of habit."** Look for an app that can redirect the habit: StillTabs adds Home Screen icons named Instagram and YouTube that open it; minulo, SocialLite and FeedOff can use Screen Time to lock the official apps.

**"I need TikTok or Snapchat."** minulo covers TikTok. Check each app's list of platforms before installing.

**"Privacy matters most."** Check the label column. FeedOff, Nope, Slowth and #blockit say Data Not Collected.

**"I'm on Android."** Most apps here are iPhone only. ScrollGuard and AppBlock have Android versions.

## FAQ

**What is the best free app to block Reels and Shorts on iPhone?**
If "free" means nothing to buy at all: StillTabs or FeedOff, which both keep your DMs, or Nope if you use Safari. minulo and Fluid Friction are free for basic blocking and sell extras.

**Can an app remove Reels inside the real Instagram app?**
Not directly; iOS doesn't allow it. Scroll Less comes closest by watching the screen through a broadcast; it also offers the websites inside its own app. The other apps show Instagram inside their own app instead.

**Do these apps see my Instagram password?**
In apps of the "own app" type you sign in on the platform's own page inside the app. Check each app's privacy policy; StillTabs, for example, runs no server in between, so passwords and messages don't pass through it.

**Is there a Reels blocker for Android?**
Yes, but most apps here are iPhone only. ScrollGuard and AppBlock have Android versions.

## About this list

This list is kept by the makers of [StillTabs](https://stilltabs.com), which is one of the apps below. We list every app that fits, competitors included, use the same columns for all of them, and fix corrections from anyone, including other developers. If something here is wrong, [open an issue](https://github.com/albertmajor/awesome-reels-shorts-blockers/issues) and we'll fix it.
